Now the idea makes sense.

Most connections can remain local while a single long-range link creates a shortcut between distant parts of the network. That is the intuition behind the small-world phenomenon studied by Watts and Strogatz.

Behind the animation

Shortest paths are calculated with Breadth-First Search (BFS). The simulator will later extend this one-shortcut experiment into the full small-world analysis using average path length and clustering.
