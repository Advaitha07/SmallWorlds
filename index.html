Now the idea makes sense.

Most connections can remain local while a few long-range links create shortcuts between distant parts of the network. That is the intuition behind the small-world phenomenon studied by Watts and Strogatz.

Behind the animation

Shortest paths are calculated with Breadth-First Search (BFS). The simulator also calculates the network's average shortest-path length. The next stage adds clustering and the full rewiring experiment.
